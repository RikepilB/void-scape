# Evidence Outreach

Outreach written from evidence, not vibes. The hook is not "I like your work"; it is a cited
moment: the claim they made at [MM:SS], the slide at [image 3], the message they sent at
[message 42].

## The 4 Golden Rules (never break these)

1. **One real hook** — never write without a specific, verifiable observation about the person,
   pulled from a Voidscape evidence bundle or a source the user provides. Cite it.
2. **One small ask** — default is a short chat or two questions. Never a referral, resume, or
   favor ask in a first message.
3. **One follow-up maximum** — if no reply after two messages, move on.
4. **Never the same text twice** — every message is unique. Platforms flag duplicate text, and
   so do humans. Structures here are shapes, not strings.

**No-hook rule:** if no verifiable hook exists, do not draft. Output: "WARNING — no evidence-
backed hook found. Read a source first (video, post, chat, audit) or add a specific detail."

## Pre-flight scan (before every message)

1. **WHO** are they? Role, company, seniority.
2. **WHAT is the hook?** A cited moment from an evidence bundle, a post they wrote, a project on
   their profile, a mutual connection, or a profile view. The evidence label goes in your notes,
   not necessarily in the message.
3. **WHAT do we share?** School, city, stack, language, industry.
4. **WHAT is the ask?** ONE low-friction ask: a 15-minute chat, or two specific questions.

## Message shapes

| Situation | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| Not connected yet | Connection note: hook + one line of relevance, under 300 characters |
| Connected | First DM within 48 hours: hook, why you, one ask |
| Very senior / async | Two questions instead of a call ask |
| Met or spoke | Thank-you same day, one concrete thing you took away |
| Acted on their advice | Loop-closer: what you did and the outcome |
| No reply in 5-7 days | One gentle nudge with two specific time windows, then stop |

## Voice rules

- Plain verbs: built, cut, reduced, shipped, led, want, bring.
- Active voice. Sentences readable aloud in one breath.
- Concrete opener about THEM; closing ends on the ask.
- No links, email addresses, or phone numbers in any first message.
- Max two questions per message.
- Never invent metrics, names, or events. Missing fact = `[VERIFY: ...]`.

## Account safety is a writing concern

Unique text every send, hook-gated messages, and human review before anything goes out. Sending
limits and pacing belong to the harness and the human pressing the button, never to this skill.

## Output format

Every draft ships with a notes block:

```text
--- NOTES ---
Hook used: [the observation + its evidence citation]
Ask: [chat / two questions / other]
Evidence: [bundle path or source the hook came from]
Placeholders to verify: [VERIFY tags, or "none"]
Alternative opener: [one alternate first sentence]
```
