## LinkedIn

I built Voidscape because I kept saving videos I meant to revisit, then losing the useful moments
inside them.

My original problem was personal. I wanted an easier way to work through Instagram Reels, YouTube
videos, and my own recordings. A title or thumbnail was never enough. I needed the actual frames,
words, and timestamps.

Voidscape accepts a local video, audio file, or supported public video URL. It prepares selected
frames, a timestamped transcript, and a manifest that an AI agent can inspect.

Before processing, it shows the scope, cost, dependencies, and privacy boundary. Cloud processing
and model downloads require separate approval.

I am building it for OpenAI Build Week in the Apps for Your Life category. Codex helped me audit
the engine, find timestamp and installer problems, and create a workflow other people can test.

The code works on my machine and against the included fixture. That does not mean the experience is
clear to someone new.

I am looking for 5 to 8 people willing to try the prototype. I want to learn where you hesitate,
what feels useful, and what you expected it to do.

Prototype showcase: [SHOWCASE_URL]

Feedback form: [FEEDBACK_FORM_URL]

Project: https://voidscape.club/

If you work with saved videos, recordings, courses, meetings, or research, I would value your
honest feedback.
