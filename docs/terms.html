1. Prototype scope

Voidscape includes a static website, an open-source command-line tool, an agent-skill bundle, compatibility files, documentation, and tests. It prepares selected frames, transcripts, and manifests from supported local media or URLs. It does not guarantee that an AI agent's later interpretation is complete or correct.

2. Responsible access

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the legal right and platform permission to access, download, transcribe, transform, and analyse each source. A working URL, browser login, cookie export, or command-line option does not create that permission.

3. Approval gates

The preview and command flags help communicate cost, privacy, and dependency choices. They are safety controls, not legal advice or a substitute for reviewing the selected provider's terms. Keep `--allow-cloud` and `--allow-model-download` off unless you understand and accept the named operation.
